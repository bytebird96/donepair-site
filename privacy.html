Privacy policy

Your work photos belong with you.

Effective September 10, 2026 · DonePair for iOS and Android
Developer: HANSAE LEE · Contact: mugy2869@gmail.com

DonePair stores job photos, notes and generated reports on your device. Advertising services and information you choose to email us are handled separately, as described below.

1. Photos, job records and reports

The app saves photos you select or capture, job titles, dates, notes, business details, checklists and generated PDF/image reports in its local app storage. We do not operate a server that receives these work records, and do not send your work photos or notes to the advertising provider. DonePair does not require an account.

Camera and photo access is used when you choose those features. You can manage permissions in your device settings. Copying a photo into DonePair does not modify the original in your photo library. Clearly labeled AI-generated photos in the guide are demonstration assets; no AI service processes your own photos.

2. Sharing, backup and deletion

Reports leave the app when you choose a destination in the system share sheet. The receiving app or service then handles that copy under its own policies. Operating-system backup and Files access may also include app documents, depending on your device settings.

You can delete individual jobs in DonePair. Removing the app may remove its local records. Copies previously shared, saved elsewhere or included in backups must be managed in those locations. Keep separate copies of important reports. We cannot restore records we do not hold.

3. Advertising and privacy choices

DonePair uses Google AdMob to display banner ads and optional rewarded ads. Watching a rewarded ad can remove the DonePair watermark for that job. Standard report creation, saving and sharing remain available without completing an ad. There are currently no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Google's mobile advertising SDK may process an IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device or app identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostics and performance information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation. We request non-personalized ads; this does not mean that the advertising SDK collects no information. We do not request access to the iOS advertising identifier through an app tracking permission prompt.

Where required, Google's consent interface provides applicable choices before advertising requests. An “Ad privacy choices” option appears in About DonePair when the consent service requires it. You can return there to revise available choices. If advertising cannot load, you can still use the core report features.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps. Google may process information outside your country under its own policies. No other advertising mediation networks are configured.

4. Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to include. We use them to answer your request and investigate the reported problem. Please do not send customer photos or sensitive information unless necessary; redact identifying details first. Email is handled by Gmail. We keep support correspondence only as needed for the request and related record-keeping, and you can ask us to delete it, subject to applicable retention obligations.

5. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies or a contact form to the site. The hosting provider may process technical connection information, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect the website. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. Clicking an email link opens your own email application.

6. Children and your choices

DonePair is a work-reporting utility intended for adults, not a service directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through support. Contact us if you believe a child has provided it.

You may contact us about access, correction or deletion of information we hold, or other privacy rights available where you live. Locally stored work records can be managed in the app; Google-managed advertising information and choices are addressed through Google's services and the in-app privacy options where available.

7. Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when data practices change. Contact mugy2869@gmail.com with privacy questions.
