For a bug report, include your device model, iOS/Android version, DonePair version and the steps that led to the issue. Remove customer details from screenshots.
How do I make my first report?

Create a new job, give it a name, and add at least one complete before/after photo pair. In Details, add notes or checklist items, then choose Create report. Use Share PDF or Images to export. You can replay the sample guide from About DonePair.

Where are my photos and records stored?

On your device in DonePair's app storage. We do not have a cloud copy of your jobs. Keep important reports separately using Share PDF and Save to Files. Deleting the app may delete local records.

Can I use the app without watching an ad?

Yes. Standard reports can be created, saved and shared without completing an ad. An optional rewarded ad removes the DonePair watermark for that job. If an ad is unavailable, you can still share the standard report.

How do I change the language?

DonePair follows your phone's supported language preferences: English, Korean, Japanese, Simplified Chinese or Traditional Chinese. Other preferences fall back to English. Your own notes stay as written; saved reports keep their original language.

Camera or photo access is not working.

Check DonePair's permissions in your device settings. You can choose existing photos from the photo library instead of using the camera. Tell us your device and operating-system version if the problem continues.

How can I delete my information?

Delete individual jobs in the app. Manage exported reports, shared copies and backups in their respective apps or services. To request deletion of support emails you sent us, contact us from that email address.

Are there subscriptions or accounts?

No. The current version does not require an account and has no subscriptions or in-app purchases. Advertising supports the app.
